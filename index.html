Update from Google Drive

Download the music and layout from the team's Google Drive folder (Hockey Music App), then open the downloads here. The app adds the newest layout and only the songs that are new or changed, so updates are quick. Nothing needs unzipping.

How to download the folder from Google Drive
  1. On a computer: go to drive.google.com and open Shared with me › Hockey Music App.
    On a tablet or phone: open drive.google.com in Chrome (or Safari on iPhone/iPad) and switch to the desktop site: Chrome menu ⋮ › Desktop site, or in Safari tap aA › Request Desktop Website. The Google Drive app can't download folders.
  2. Tap ⋮ next to the Hockey Songs folder (or right-click it) and choose Download. Google zips it up: it arrives as one or more zip files (…-001.zip, …-002.zip). Download all of them.
  3. Tap ⋮ next to Hockey-Game-Day.rinkside.json (the layout) and choose Download. You don't need the .rinkpack or .html files.
  4. When the downloads finish, tap Open Drive download here and pick all of the files together (they're in Downloads, or Files › Downloads on iPhone).
  5. Check what the app found, then tap Add to this device. Afterwards you can delete the downloaded files to free up space.

Next time, download just the layout file and any song folders that changed. Downloading Hockey Songs again is fine too: songs already on this device are skipped.

    Help

    Running a game (volunteers)

    • Pick the team at the top left. The big GOAL! button, Pregame and Game music follow the team.
    • Tap a button to play it. Tap it again to fade it out.
    • Fade out all (big red button) when the puck drops. Stop cuts everything instantly.
    • Game music: the first tap starts the team's playlist. Every tap after that plays the next song.
    • Random buttons (Penalty, Icing, …) pick a song that hasn't played yet this game.
    • New game (tap twice) clears the “Played” marks before puck drop.
    • Lock hides settings and editing. Press and hold the lock for a second and a half to unlock.

    Questions

    How do I get the newest songs and layout?

    From the team's Google Drive folder (Hockey Music App, shared with you by the organizer), download the Hockey Songs folder and the layout file (Hockey-Game-Day.rinkside.json). Then unlock, open Settings, tap Open Drive download and pick all the downloaded files. The app shows what's new; tap Add to this device. Only new or changed songs are copied.

    Settings has step-by-step instructions under “How to download the folder from Google Drive”. On a phone or tablet, use the browser's desktop site: the Google Drive app can't download whole folders.

    How do I publish an update for everyone? (organizer)

    New songs: put the MP3 in the right category folder inside Hockey Songs in the team's Google Drive folder. To give it a button, open the app on your computer, tap Edit, open the tab and use Add songs to pick that same file (from Google Drive on your computer, or a copy with the same name).

    New layout: after editing, open Settings and tap Save layout file. Put the file (it ends in .rinkside.json) in the Hockey Music App folder, replacing the old one, and keep the name Hockey-Game-Day.rinkside.json so volunteers know which file to download.

    Everyone gets the changes the next time they download from Drive and open the files in the app.

    How do I add a new song?

    Unlock, tap Edit, open the tab you want, and tap Add songs. Pick one or more MP3 files. Each file becomes a button named after the file. Tap the new button to rename it, set its colour and set its start point. Tap Done at the top when you're finished.

    How do I make a song start at the good part?

    In Edit, tap the button, then Play from 0:00. When the part you want hits, tap Set start here. Tap Listen from start point to check it. For a short clip, also tap Set stop here where it should end. You can type exact seconds instead.

    How do I change or replace a song on a button?

    In Edit, tap the button and use Choose a different file. The button keeps its name, colour and place. Check the start point afterwards, because the new file may be different.

    How do the Game Day groups (Penalty, Icing…) work?

    They come from button names. The Penalty group plays a random song from every button whose name starts with “Penalty”, on any tab. To add a song to a group, rename its button, for example “Penalty - Sabotage - Beastie Boys”. To remove it, rename it without the word at the start.

    To make a new group, go to Game Day, tap Edit, then Edit groups next to Whistles, Moments or Game over. Give it a label and the starting text to match. The count next to each group shows how many songs it found.

    How do I change the goal horn or a team's playlists?

    In Edit, tap the team name at the top (or the big GOAL! button). Choose the goal horn, the pregame playlist and the game music playlist. Only buttons with “Goal Horn” in their name show up as goal horns.

    How do I make or change a playlist?

    In Edit, open a tab and tap Add playlist, or tap an existing playlist button. Search for songs and tap them to add. Use the arrows to change the order and ✕ to remove a song. The songs stay on their own tabs too.

    How do I add, rename or reorder tabs?

    In Edit, tap New tab at the end of the tab row. To change a tab, open it and tap Tab settings: rename it, pick a colour, move it left or right, add a whole folder of songs, or delete it.

    How do I move or copy a button to another tab?

    In Edit, tap the button. Change Tab to move it, or use Also put a copy on to show it in two places. Use Move earlier and Move later to change its position.

    How do I share this with another volunteer or tablet?

    Open Settings, then Share and back up.

    Layout file: small, easy to email. The other person opens the app, taps Open a layout file, then adds their own copy of the music with Add music folder. Songs match by folder and file name, so keep the same file names.

    Show pack: includes the music, so it is large. Copy it with a USB stick, Google Drive or a cable. On the other tablet, tap Open a layout file or show pack and everything is ready.

    Save a layout file after big changes. It is also your backup.

    Can I edit on my computer instead of the tablet?

    Yes. Open the app in Chrome on your computer, make your changes, then save a layout file (or a show pack) and open it on the tablet.

    Some buttons have dashed outlines and say “File not loaded”.

    That song file isn't on this device. Open Settings and add your music folder again, or open the button in Edit and choose the file. Settings lists every missing song.

    How do I put it on the home screen with the hockey icon?

    Open the app in Chrome, tap Chrome's menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen (or Install app). The crossed-sticks icon appears on the home screen.

    If you opened the single file, the icon has a small Chrome badge and the app opens with Chrome's address bar. Tap the full screen button (four corners) at the top to hide the address bar. Swipe from the edge or press back to leave full screen.

    If you opened it from its web address, the app installs like a real app: no Chrome badge and no address bar.

    Can I use it on an iPhone or iPad?

    Yes, from its web address in Safari (the single downloaded file can't run on an iPhone or iPad). Open the address, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and always open it from that icon: Safari can clear a website's saved music if it isn't on the Home Screen.

    The easiest way to load music is to download Hockey Songs and the layout file from Google Drive in Safari (tap aA › Request Desktop Website first), then use Settings › Open Drive download. You can also use Add music folder (iOS 18.4 or newer) or Add music files, or open a show pack made on another device. If there's no sound while the ring/silent switch is on silent, update iOS.

    The first download is about 2 GB, and the songs then take up the same again in the app until you delete the zip files. Make sure there's room, and keep the screen on (Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock) until it finishes.

    Make show packs on an Android tablet or a computer. Saving a multi-gigabyte pack on an iPhone or iPad may not work.

    The tablet screen turns off or locks during the game.

    Change the tablet's own setting before the game: Settings > Display > Screen timeout, and pick the longest time (often 10 or 30 minutes). Some tablets also have Settings > Developer options > Stay awake, which keeps the screen on while it's charging.

    The sound is a little late on the Bluetooth speaker.

    Bluetooth always adds a short delay. Tap goal horns as soon as the goal is scored. A cable to the sound system has no delay.

    Nothing plays.

    Check the tablet volume and the Master slider. Make sure the Bluetooth speaker is connected before opening the app. Buttons with dashed outlines don't have their song file on this device yet.

    I made a mess. Can I undo?

    Open a layout file you saved earlier (Settings, then Open a layout file). Or use Restore the built-in show to go back to the show this copy came with. Your music stays on the device either way.
